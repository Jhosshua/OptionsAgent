OptionsAgent trades a defined-risk options framework on a $100,000 Alpaca paper account (re-funded Aug 28, 2026). The system runs two completely isolated engines: Engine 1 (Credit-Spread Seller) sells 30 to 45 DTE vertical spreads across 13 liquid underlyings under $50, where Claude proposes directional conviction while fixed Python rails select all strikes, deltas, and sizing; and Engine 2 (0DTE ORB Scalper) trades pure momentum breakouts on SPY and QQQ intraday with zero LLM involvement.
Engine 1 Execution Pipeline (Daily Seller, 10:15 to 10:27 ET) Deterministic Rails and Winner Gate
STEP 01
Cron Wakes It
Runs weekdays 10:15 to 10:27 ET via local crontab with an atomic once-per-day lock file. Fails closed if the broker clock check fails or market is closed.
STEP 02
Chain Snapshot
Archives full option chains (quotes, IV, greeks) for all 13 watchlist names to disk and builds a structured market context bundle per symbol.
STEP 03
Claude Proposes
Local Claude Code CLI (sonnet, no tools, no web) proposes only ticker, direction, conviction (0 to 1), and thesis. Strikes and size are strictly banned.
STEP 04
Rails Sizing Gate
Conviction floor 0.60 (below = veto), max 6 positions, phase allowlist. Sizing scales linearly from 30% to 100% of free options buying power.
STEP 05
Contract Selection
Short leg 15 to 30 delta, width capped at $2.00, 30 to 45 DTE. Put spread if bullish, call spread if bearish. Single MLEG combo limit order (prefix oa-).
STEP 06
Winner Profile Gate
Spread must match replayed winners: CCL put (credit ≥ $0.29, width ≥ $1.50), SOFI put (credit ≥ $0.23, width ≥ $1.00), or F call (credit ≥ $0.06, width ≤ $0.50).
Engine 1 Exit Sweep (Every 20 Min During Market Hours, No LLM) SELLER EXITS
Profit target at 50% of credit received; stop loss at 2x credit evaluated only from 10:00 ET and requiring two consecutive sweeps to confirm (protects against transient wide quotes); mandatory force close at 21 DTE; and proactive dividend checks closing short calls prior to ex-dates. Worst case per spread is width minus credit (~$200), bounding 6 positions near $1,200 (1.2% account risk).
Engine 2 0DTE ORB Scalper (Armed Aug 28, 12:25 ET, No LLM) SCALPER INTRADAY
Fully isolated (own state, registry, prefix oas-). Trades 0DTE ATM SPY and QQQ on 09:30 to 09:33 ET opening range breakout. Requires 1-min bar close outside range with volume ≥ 1.5x session avg, confirmed by next bar and VWAP. Fixed $250/trade, max 2/day, 1 open, no entries at/after 11:30 ET. Exits: +50% target, -30% stop (-60% if thesis holds), 15-min theta cut, mandatory 15:50 ET flatten. Split feeds: SIP data key plus Public.com chain sidecar.

Q1Is this real money?
No. OptionsAgent operates strictly on an Alpaca paper account ($100,000 balance, re-funded Aug 28, 2026). The Python broker client validates environment keys at startup and unconditionally refuses non-paper endpoints. No live capital is ever at risk.
Q2What exactly does the LLM control?
The local Claude Code CLI (sonnet) acts solely as a directional idea generator. It receives an isolated JSON bundle and proposes only {underlying, direction, conviction, thesis}. Output is schema-validated; the LLM cannot name strikes, deltas, or sizes. Parsing failure yields zero trades. The scalper uses no LLM at all.
Q3Why credit spreads?
Day-one operational testing with a single-leg MARA long put suffered severe theta decay, losing 37.6% of premium. The architecture pivoted to defined-risk credit spreads, capturing positive time decay while capping maximum loss to spread width minus credit.
Q4How does sizing work?
Position budget is a conviction-scaled share of available options buying power. At the 0.60 conviction floor, sizing receives 30% of free BP, scaling linearly to 100% at 0.85+. Contracts equal budget divided by (width × 100). If budget yields under 1 contract, the trade is skipped.
Q5100% of buying power sounds aggressive.
It is deliberate operator policy: "use all the cash, not necessarily all at once." Structural brakes prevent reckless exposure: $2.00 maximum spread width (~$200 loss cap), 6-position ceiling, broker BP rejection, and an optional tighten-only kill switch (OA_MAX_POSITION_USD).
Q6What is the winner profile gate?
Historical replay of July fills isolated three profitable setups: CCL bullish puts (credit ≥ $0.29, width ≥ $1.50), SOFI bullish puts (credit ≥ $0.23, width ≥ $1.00), and F bearish calls (credit ≥ $0.06, width ≤ $0.50). All other proposals fail closed. This is a deliberate in-sample overfit frozen to gather clean out-of-sample evidence.
Q7Walk me through the exits.
Seller: 20-min sweeps without LLM enforce ex-dividend risk checks first, 50% profit target, 2x credit stop (evaluated from 10:00 ET and confirmed across 2 consecutive sweeps to prevent bad-tick stops), and 21 DTE force close. Scalper: +50% target, -30% stop (-60% if breakout intact), 15-min theta cut, and mandatory 15:50 ET flatten.
Q8Assignment and overnight gap risk?
Verticals are defined-risk: max loss is width minus credit regardless of assignment mechanics. The 2x stop is a loss control, not a guarantee. An overnight gap through short strikes costs the full width (~$200/spread). Six positions bound worst-case catastrophic loss near $1,200 (1.2% of account). Scalper never holds overnight.
Q9Is there a daily loss limit?
Seller: no dedicated loss cap, as risk is bounded structurally by the $2 width limit, 6 positions, single entry window, and winner gate. Scalper: strictly bounded to 2 trades of $250 each ($500 max daily risk before exits). An emergency halt can be re-enabled via environment variables.
Q10What does the 0DTE scalper actually do?
Engine 2 is a fully isolated system (prefix oas-, zero LLM) trading 0DTE ATM SPY and QQQ. It marks the 09:30 to 09:33 ET opening range, entering on 1-min breakout with volume ≥ 1.5x avg confirmed by the next bar and VWAP. $250/trade, max 2/day, 1 open, no entries ≥ 11:30 ET (12 of 12 historical late entries lost), mandatory flatten at 15:50 ET.
Q11How do I know what it did on any given day?
Through the read-only dashboard at 127.0.0.1:8765 and structured JSONL logs: data/decisions.jsonl (every proposal and veto reason), data/structures.jsonl (open/closed credit spreads), plus data/scalp_decisions.jsonl and data/scalp_positions.jsonl for intraday scalps.
Q12What stops a bug from doing something catastrophic?
Hard-coded Python rails (env can only tighten constraints), broker client refusing non-paper endpoints, single locked daily entry window, exit sweeps reconciling local registry against broker positions with alerts on vanished legs, and scalper force-flattening at 15:50 ET while auto-recovering orphaned positions.
