The rules and the FAQ

Deterministic guardrails, in-sample winner filters, and architectural answers.

Watchlist (13 Liquid Underlyings)

Selected for low share price, high liquidity, and tight bid-ask spreads.
The Four "Never" Rules
  • Invariant Never let model output pick a strike, delta, or expiration.
  • Invariant Never loosen a rail by editing config (env vars may only tighten).
  • Invariant Never place a live-money order.
  • Invariant Never buy options after an extended move (anti-chase rule).
1. Is this real money?
No, Alpaca paper only. The code refuses non-paper endpoints at startup and connection time.
2. What exactly does the LLM control?
The idea only (ticker, direction, conviction, thesis). Output is schema-checked; malformed data is dropped. The scalper uses no LLM at all.
3. Why credit spreads?
Day one a MARA long put lost 37.6% of its premium in a session. The bot pivoted to selling defined-risk premium with a width-capped worst case.
4. How does sizing work?
Conviction-scaled share of free buying power; 30% at the 0.60 floor to 100% at 0.85+. If sizing falls below one contract, the trade is skipped.
5. 100% of buying power sounds aggressive.
Deliberate policy. The brakes are the $2 width cap, 6 positions, broker margin rejection, and a tighten-only kill switch (OA_MAX_POSITION_USD).
6. What is the winner profile gate?
Three replayed winners (CCL put spread, SOFI put spread, F call spread), hard-coded and fail-closed. A frozen in-sample profile held unchanged to collect honest out-of-sample evidence.
7. Walk me through the exits.
Seller: 50% profit, 2x credit stop confirmed across two sweeps starting 10:00 ET, 21 DTE close, dividend check. Scalper: +50% target, -30% or -60% stop, 15-minute theta cut, 15:50 flatten.
8. Assignment and overnight gap risk?
Verticals are defined risk; max loss is width minus credit. The stop is loss control, not a guarantee. Worst day near $1,200. The scalper never holds overnight.
9. Is there a daily loss limit?
The seller has none, relying on structural position brakes. The scalper is bounded at 2 x $250 before exits, with an optional emergency halt switch.
10. What does the 0DTE scalper actually do?
Watches SPY and QQQ 09:30 to 09:33 range. Enters on 1-minute close outside range on 1.5x volume, confirmed by next bar and VWAP. Buys $250 ATM contracts, exiting on targets, stops, 15-minute theta, or 15:50 ET.
11. How do I know what it did on any given day?
Read-only local dashboard at 127.0.0.1:8765, plus data/decisions.jsonl and data/structures.jsonl for the seller, data/scalp_decisions.jsonl and data/scalp_positions.jsonl for the scalper.
12. What stops a bug from doing something catastrophic?
Rails hard-coded in Python, env may only tighten. Broker client refuses non-paper, entry windows are locked, sweeps reconcile live registries, and the scalper force-flattens at 15:50 ET and re-adopts orphans.
